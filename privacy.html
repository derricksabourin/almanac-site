Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy covers Almanac Studio, the internal tool The Almanac (Grey Almanac and Fortune Almanac) uses to publish videos to its own YouTube channels, and this website.

YouTube API Services

Almanac Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data we access

Almanac Studio is used only by The Almanac's own team, signed in to The Almanac's own Google account. With that account's permission it can:

  • upload videos, titles, descriptions and tags to The Almanac's own YouTube channels, and
  • read basic information about those channels (such as the channel name and ID) to confirm it is posting to the right one.

It does not access, collect, store or share any information about viewers, commenters or any other YouTube users.

How data is stored

The authorization token that lets Almanac Studio upload to our channels is stored as an encrypted secret in our private build system. Video files and their text are stored in our private project storage before publishing. We do not sell or share this data with anyone.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking, and does not collect personal information.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, we delete the stored token.
